# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers coordinating coding agents across local, folder-workspace, worktree, SSH, and paired mobile workflows.

## Product Purpose

h0x-ADE keeps agent work visible, controllable, and recoverable while framing the developer's own tools instead of competing with them.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and dependable.

## Anti-references

Avoid ornamental SaaS styling, decorative color, unfamiliar controls, inconsistent platform identity, and branding that competes with working content.

## Design Principles

- Let the developer's work remain visually primary.
- Prefer familiar, consistent controls over novelty.
- Use brand treatments deliberately and consistently across platforms.
- Make state, recovery, and remote uncertainty explicit.
- Preserve compatibility without exposing legacy identity in normal output.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard operation, platform conventions, high-contrast legibility, reduced-motion behavior, and accessible labels across desktop and mobile.
